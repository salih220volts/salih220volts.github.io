Perfecting the work is better than the work itself.

Background

I'm a 4th-year electrical engineering student — but I didn't wait for graduation to start working. I've freelanced on real projects, worked in the field, and learned how the market actually operates: what clients need, what deadlines demand, and what separates a working system from a textbook diagram. That experience puts me ahead of where most fresh graduates or junior engineers start.

Approach

I don't just draft — I plan smart. Before any line is drawn, I map out requirements, anticipate conflicts between systems, and sequence the design so changes later don't cascade into rework.

Tools & Skills

  • Design software — AutoCAD 2022 (Electrical), DIALux evo, ETAP
  • Engineering fundamentals — Load calculations & demand factors, three-phase power systems & transformer configurations, cable sizing & voltage drop analysis, panel schedule design, single-line diagrams (SLD)
  • Systems expertise — Lighting design, power distribution, low-current systems (fire alarm, CCTV, access control, data/telephone)
  • Documentation & planning — BOQ preparation, Excel-based schedules, Microsoft Project scheduling
  • Software & scripting — Python, HTML/CSS/JS website development
  • Standards awareness — Familiar with IEC 60364 and the Saudi Electrical Code (SBC 401), alongside Egyptian code-compliant design practices

What Sets Me Apart

Freelance experience across both electrical engineering and software — a combination that lets me build tools, automate reporting, and think about systems the way a programmer does, not just a drafter.
